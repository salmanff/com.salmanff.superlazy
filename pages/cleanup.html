Settings — model, windows and thresholds; tuned here against real data, saved as your defaults
What is always sent: the whole map — every active project with its open to-dos and its last few closed ones, the people you deal with, your standing instructions with usage counts, your situation text. The windows only decide the history that goes with it: the input window is how many days of incoming items (mail, chat, notes, agent work) are listed for the model to read against the map; the hand-edits window is how many days of your own manual changes the rules/learn sections look at for patterns; the focus horizon is how far ahead the Focus section plans. Thresholds are all ANDs: a to-do is "stale" when it is open AND its importance is at most the limit AND it is older than the age AND nothing has touched it for the quiet days; a project is an "archive candidate" when it has no open items AND has been quiet for the months given.
