While recording is ON, every LLM exchange the per-message engine makes (background job or foreground check) is stored verbatim — the turns sent, the reply, the provider's usage and prices — and pruned after the retention window. Toggle it here or from the Setup page console: superlazy.audit(true) / superlazy.audit(false).
