superlazy
A to-do list that keeps itself up to date

superlazy reads your email, Slack conversations and AI coding-agent sessions, and keeps a to-do list for you: what you owe, what others owe you and what your team owes. It creates to-dos, assigns them and crosses them off as the messages come in.

It has no backend of its own: your mail goes only through your freezr server and your own LLM account.
